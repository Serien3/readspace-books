# Notes on Structured Programming

**NOTES ON STRUCTURED PROGRAMMING · EWD249**

Author: Prof. dr. Edsger W. Dijkstra  
Institution: Technological University Eindhoven, The Netherlands, Department of Mathematics  
Report: T.H. Report 70-WSK-03  
Original manuscript: August 1969; second edition: April 1970

Source: [EWD249 scanned edition](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/ewd02xx/EWD249.PDF); this edition is based on the [English transcription](../Notes-on-Structured-Programming.md).

> **Editorial arrangement**: This English edition is arranged for paragraph-by-paragraph comparison with the Chinese edition in `../sections-zh/`. Repeated title pages are merged; chapter and subsection boundaries, paragraphs, lists, tables, code blocks, displayed formulas and figures follow the Chinese edition. English source wording is retained wherever possible; connecting prose around formulas is regrouped where needed. Subsection titles, translator’s notes and the glossary are editorial additions corresponding to the Chinese edition, not part of Dijkstra’s original text. Code keeps the ALGOL-style notation: `≔` means assignment, `=` equality, `non` logical negation, and `if … do …` conditional execution. Formulas and explicitly noted corrections follow the Chinese edition. Figures are referenced in `../figures/`.
